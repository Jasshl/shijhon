How following works

Shijhon follows the artists you favorited in your apps and the artists of your favorite albums and songs.{% if most_played %} It also follows the {{ most_played }} artists with the most plays among the albums you played in the last {{ played_days }} days.{% endif %}

Once a day it asks the catalog for their releases. A release that came out in the last {{ days }} days and is not in your library shows first among the newest albums in your apps, and on this page. It leaves the list once it is in your library, or when it is older than that.

Nothing is added to your library until you play, favorite or add a release. Other accounts choose for themselves, and nobody else sees your artists or your list. Turning this off removes both.
