Library

Complete the albums you own only part of.

How albums are completed

When the catalog has a release that matches an album you own part of, the album is shown complete in your apps: the missing songs appear as catalog songs. Filling adds them to your library as placeholders. The policy decides which albums are filled automatically; the others are filled when one of their songs is played, starred or added to a playlist.

Library pass

Checks every album against the catalog in the background.

What the modes do

Dry run fills nothing automatically and lists what it would fill. On fills the albums that meet the policy. In both modes the pass also reads the track lists of releases already in your library again from time to time: On adds tracks the catalog has added since and corrects the lengths of songs that have no audio yet, and a dry run only counts them. Off stops the pass: albums are matched, and filled if they meet the policy, when an app views them. A change to or from Off applies after a restart; until then Off works as a dry run.
