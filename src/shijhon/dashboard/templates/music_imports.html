How importing works

You choose files or a folder, they upload and are checked, and you approve which songs to import. Nothing is imported before you approve.

Imported songs are added to Original imported library and All. Your apps keep using {{ active.name }} on every device.

To take an import back out of the library, open it from Your imports below and choose Review removal. Removal can be undone with Restore.

If this tab closes, reopen this batch under Your imports and choose Resume upload. Select the same files or folder, then Continue upload.
