Playlists

Playlists are published to Navidrome and show in music apps. AI agents, descriptions, imports and discovery mixes can make them.

How playlists work

Each playlist is published to your Navidrome account, so it shows up in your music app. Songs it needs that are not in your library yet can be added from the catalog as placeholders or as missing tracks of an album you have; publishing does not download audio. Audio is fetched when you play or download a song, through your add-ons.

A connected AI agent makes playlists with its own model. Described playlists use server AI. Discovery mixes use Last.fm listening history and can also use server AI. Imports match an existing list.
