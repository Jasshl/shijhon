Playlists

Make playlists for your music app: describe one, import one, or build a discovery mix from your Last.fm history.

How playlists work

Each playlist is published to your Navidrome account, so it shows up in your music app. Songs it needs that are not in your library yet can be added from the catalog as placeholders or as missing tracks of an album you have; publishing does not download audio. Audio is fetched when you play or download a song, through your add-ons.

Describing a playlist uses the AI service. Discovery mixes use your Last.fm listening history and can use AI too. Imports match a list you already have.
