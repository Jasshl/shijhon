How described playlists work

Connect your Navidrome account on Playlists and choose the shared AI service. Your listening history and your library are optional influences; the exact recording lookup still checks your library when library context is off.

Regular refreshes need saved instructions and AI. Publishing can add whole catalog releases as placeholders; it does not download audio. Audio is fetched through normal playback and download rules.
