How add-ons are used

Songs people are listening to follow the plan below. Add-ons that are off are skipped. Fetches that nobody is waiting on, like the next songs in a queue, go through the same steps one add-on at a time, so they don't ask several add-ons for the same song.

Downloads and playlist preparation use one selected add-on at a time for each song and share transfer slots. Set the defaults on Playback; set an add-on's own limits and shared account or worker group under its Settings, in Advanced settings.

    How a song starts

    Add-ons in the same step start together, and the first to send audio plays.

    How the plan works

    Each step has a time. When it ends without audio, or every add-on in it has failed or lacks the song, the next step's add-ons join, and the ones already asked keep trying: the first to send audio plays, and the others are stopped. Downloads and songs prepared ahead ask one add-on at a time instead, each for its time for a download. Apps stop waiting at {{ plan.wait|number }} s, so steps after that are never reached, and their add-ons wait in a list under the board.{% if not plan.readonly %} Pull an add-on's bar sideways into another step's column to start with it, or onto the line between two columns for a step of its own. Under the board, pull an add-on up into a column, or up and down in that list. Pull a step's edge to change its time; when a step is too narrow to show its edge, a tap on its number or on one of its bars shows it.{% endif %}
