Get a playlist's audio ready on the server, then download it to your device with your music app.

How preparing works

Preparing fetches each missing song's audio through the add-ons into the server library. It prepares server files only; your music app still saves its own copies on your device. The song list is fixed when you start: later edits to the playlist don't change it.

Cancelling keeps completed audio and lets current songs finish. Unfinished work pauses after a restart until you resume it. Automatic cleanup can remove prepared audio.
