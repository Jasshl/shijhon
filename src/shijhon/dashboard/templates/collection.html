How libraries work

A library is the music your apps show. The library you use in your apps applies to your account on every device; other accounts keep their own.

  • All: everything you collected - Original imported library and Shijhon library together. Your apps use All unless you choose another library.
  • Original imported library: the music you imported, the albums of the playlists you imported and your favorites.
  • Shijhon library: the albums Shijhon added from the catalog for you - what your apps played, favorited, rated, put into a playlist, downloaded, shared or bookmarked, the audio you prepared on the Downloads page and the playlists Shijhon made for you.
  • Custom libraries: selections of your own; they change only when you change them (an album in them still takes in songs added to it later).

Albums are always whole. Add or remove albums and songs here: a song you add brings its whole album, and what you remove stays out of that library, whatever would bring it in. Another account's music, plays and choices never change your libraries. Choosing what to browse here leaves the library your apps use unchanged.

Not in this library shows the music you collected that the library does not hold.{% if listing.get('legacy_shared') %} Existing shared originals count as everyone's imports until personal ownership is reviewed.{% endif %} Everything on the server also shows music other accounts added, to add to your library; it is not a library your apps can use.
